Kaicheng Luo is a PhD student in MIT's Department of Economics. His research interests lie in political economy, environmental economics, industrial organization, and development economics. He works on projects spanning policy learning, innovation, emigration, censorship, and pollution mitigation. Prior to joining MIT, Kaicheng worked as a predoctoral fellow for David Y. Yang at Harvard Economics. He received his bachelor's degree in Philosophy, Politics, and Economics (PPE) from Peking University.
